# silverbullet-tlo

A SilverBullet plug that integrates tinylittleoffice documents ([.tlo format](https://github.com/aboard-io/tinylittleoffice/blob/main/docs/tlo.md)).

`tlo` codefences:

```tlo
---
title: Neu: Dokument 2026-11-06 Nr. 1
created: 2026-11-06T21:36Z
app: write
---
Hello world!
```

Will be rendered as wordprocessor within a silverbullet page.
